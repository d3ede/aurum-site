# Asset provenance

- `public/assets/aurum.svg`: approved Aurum logo from the launcher repository.
- `src/Patterns.tsx`: original procedural vector patterns copied from the launcher's `src/components/TerrainPattern.tsx`.
- `public/assets/demo-home-light.png` and `demo-home-dark.png`: newly captured English launcher UI with isolated demonstration fixtures (Player account, Survival and Creative instances). No personal launcher data is read.

## Loader marks

These official marks identify supported loaders. Their geometry and ownership are preserved; CSS applies a muted monochrome treatment at rest and restores their colors on hover.

- Vanilla: [Minecraft website favicon](https://www.minecraft.net/etc.clientlibs/minecraftnet/clientlibs/clientlib-site/resources/favicon-96x96.png), Mojang/Microsoft. Vanilla has no separate loader logo.
- Fabric: [official Fabric logo](https://fabricmc.net/assets/logo.png). Fabric website content is under [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://fabricmc.net/), unless otherwise indicated.
- Forge: the anvil path extracted unchanged from the [official Forge logo SVG](https://files.minecraftforge.net/static/images/logo.svg); the wordmark is omitted for a compact icon.
- NeoForge: [official documentation logo](https://docs.neoforged.net/img/logo.svg), NeoForged.
- Quilt: [official logo SVG](https://github.com/QuiltMC/quiltmc.org/blob/main/public/assets/img/logo.svg), QuiltMC.

These third-party marks are not relicensed under the website's PolyForm license and do not imply affiliation or endorsement.
